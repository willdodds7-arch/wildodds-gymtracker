Delete your Wild Odds Gym Tracker account

Deleting your account is immediate and permanent. It removes your profile, your synced programs and workout history, and your usage analytics from our servers. There is no grace period and the data cannot be recovered afterwards.

If you have the app installed (fastest)

Open the app, then go to Profile → the gear icon → Settings → Account → Delete account. You'll type DELETE to confirm and re-enter your password, and you can choose whether to also erase the copy of your training data stored on your device.

If you no longer have the app

Email the request address below from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We'll verify it's you and delete the account and all associated data within 30 days (usually much sooner), then confirm by reply.

Request address: wildoddssupport [at] gmail.com

What gets deleted

  • Your account (sign-in identity).
  • Your profile (username, consent record).
  • Your synced training data (programs, sessions, exercises, workout logs, sets, completions).
  • Your usage analytics events.

Training data stored only on your own device is under your control — the in-app flow lets you erase it too, or you can simply uninstall the app.
