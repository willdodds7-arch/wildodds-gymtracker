Delete your Wild Odds Gym Tracker data

The app has no accounts and no server. Everything it stores lives only on your phone, so we never hold a copy of your data.

In the app: Settings → Delete all my data. This erases every program, workout log, note and setting on that phone immediately and permanently.
Without the app: uninstalling it erases all of its data from your phone.

If you'd still like written confirmation, or have any question about your data, email wildoddssupport@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and we'll reply within 30 days.
