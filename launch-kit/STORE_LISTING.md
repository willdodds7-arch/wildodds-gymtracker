## Full description (≤ 4000 chars)

Wild Odds Gym Tracker is a free and open-source strength-training tracker that runs entirely on your
phone. No account. No cloud. No ads. Your programs and workout logs live only on your
device — so you can track a coach's or client's programming without handing it to anyone else.

It's open source under the MIT license: you can read exactly what it does and build it yourself.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• All training data is stored in a local database on your device.
• There is no account and no server — we can't see your data because it never reaches us.
• The app has no internet permission at all: nothing can leave your phone, not even from a library.
• No ads, no analytics, no advertising IDs, no pop-ups, no payments.

UNIVERSAL PROGRAM IMPORT
• Turn any written program into a structured, trackable one in seconds — paste text, or pick a
  screenshot, photo, PDF, Excel/Google Sheets export, Word document or CSV. Text in images is read
  on your phone.
• Understands how lifters actually write programs: 3x8, 8-12, 5/3/1, 10/8/6, @RPE8, @75%, 1x5+ @85%
  TM, tempo 31X0, supersets, EMOM, timed and distance work.
• A review-and-confirm step shows a confidence score and flags anything uncertain instead of quietly
  guessing — nothing is fabricated, and the original text is preserved.
• Reads programs in English, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese and Italian.

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO TRAIN
• Fast set logging: weight and reps per set, RPE and %1RM, supersets, unilateral (left/right) modes.
• Rest timer, plate calculator, and 1RM calculator.
• Multi-week, multi-phase programs and blocks, a program builder with 12-week linear progression, and
  a curated built-in catalogue.
• Coach notes from the program, plus your own athlete notes that carry over session to session.
• Reorder your training days without losing any logged progress.
• Trends, estimated 1RMs, and a profile with your maxes and achievements.
• Optional Health Connect to show heart rate alongside a session (stays on your device).
• Smooth on 120 Hz displays.

FREE AND OPEN
• 100% free, no in-app purchases, no subscription.
• MIT-licensed open source — inspect it, fork it, contribute.

Perfect for coaches who want to load a client's block onto a device, and for lifters who want a
private, no-nonsense log they fully control.
