# Support

**Last updated 5 October 2026**

Need help with Wild Odds Gym Tracker? Here's how to get it and where to find common answers.

## Contact

Email **wildoddssupport@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you. Including your app version (shown at
the bottom of Settings) helps us help you faster.

## Common topics

- **No account needed.** The app works fully offline and keeps your training on your device. It has
  no internet access at all.
- **Moving to a new phone or reinstalling.** Settings → Export data → Export as JSON, keep the file,
  then on the new install use Settings → Export data → Restore from backup.
- **Exporting your data.** Settings → Export data saves CSV or JSON copies of your training.
- **Deleting your data.** Settings → Delete all my data erases everything on this device. There is
  no server copy, so nothing else needs deleting — but email us if you'd like confirmation.
- **Reminders.** Off by default. Every reminder has a "Turn off reminders" button.
- **Accessibility.** See Settings → Accessibility statement, and tell us what isn't working for you.
