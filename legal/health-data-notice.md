# Health & Fitness Data Notice

**Version 1.0 — Last updated 19 July 2026**

Wild Odds Gym Tracker records training information that is health-adjacent. This notice explains,
in plain terms, what that data is and how we handle it. It supplements the Privacy Policy.

## What health-adjacent data the app handles

- **Your logged training:** exercises, sets, weights, reps, RPE, %1RM, per-side values, and any
  notes you write, plus which sessions you completed and when.
- **Optional heart-rate data** *only if* you connect a wearable through Android's Health Connect.
  Heart-rate data read from Health Connect is used on your device to show session summaries; it is
  not required and is under your control in Health Connect's own settings.

## What syncs to your account

Your logged training data (above) is stored on your device and backed up to your account so it can
sync across your devices. It is protected by row-level security so only your account can read it.

## What we never do with it

- We **never sell** your health or fitness data.
- We **never share** it for advertising or use it to build advertising profiles.
- It is **not** used for any purpose other than providing the app to you (planning, logging, and
  syncing your training).

## Analytics never include it

If you opt in to usage analytics, those events are coarse and non-identifying. They **never**
contain your weights, reps, notes, heart rate, or any other training measurement — only things like
which screen was opened or whether a feature is on or off.

## Deleting it

- **Export** a full copy anytime: Settings → Account → Export my data.
- **Delete everything** anytime: Settings → Account → Delete account removes your synced training
  data (and everything else) immediately and irreversibly. You can also choose to erase the copy
  stored on your device, or simply uninstall the app.
- Heart-rate access can be revoked at any time from within Health Connect.
