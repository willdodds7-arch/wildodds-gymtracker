# Health & Fitness Data Notice

**Version 2.0 — Last updated 5 October 2026**

Wild Odds Gym Tracker records training information that is health-adjacent. This notice explains,
in plain terms, what that data is and how it is handled. It supplements the Privacy Policy.

## What health-adjacent data the app handles

- **Your logged training:** exercises, sets, weights, reps, RPE, %1RM, per-side values, and any
  notes you write, plus which sessions you completed and when.
- **Optional heart rate** *only if* you connect Health Connect and grant it. It is shown with your
  session and its summary, on your device.
- **Optional workout write-back:** if you turn it on, completed sessions are saved to Health Connect
  so your other health apps can see them.

The app does **not** request sleep, resting heart rate, HRV or any other Health Connect data.

## Where it lives

**Only on your device.** There is no account and no server, so it is never uploaded by the app and
we never see it.

## What we never do with it

- We **never sell** your health or fitness data.
- We **never share** it for advertising or use it to build advertising profiles.
- It is **not** used for anything other than showing you your own training.

## Deleting it

- **Export** a copy anytime: Settings → Export data.
- **Delete everything:** Settings → Delete all my data, or uninstall the app.
- Heart-rate access can be revoked at any time in Health Connect's own settings.
