## The Verified Creator subscription

- **Price: A$2.00 per month**, in Australian dollars, billed monthly. Where we say "less than
  7¢ a day", that is the same A$2.00 monthly charge expressed per day — you are billed monthly,
  not daily.
- The subscription **auto-renews every month** on your billing date until you cancel. Your
  billing date is the calendar day you first subscribed; Stripe emails a receipt each cycle.
- Payment is processed by **Stripe**. We never see or store your card number.
- Prices are **GST-inclusive where applicable**.

## What you get

While your subscription is active: the blue Verified Creator badge next to your name across the
app, and the ability to publish and sell training programs in the marketplace (after completing
Stripe payout onboarding and accepting the Creator Agreement).

## Cancelling

Cancel any time from **Manage subscription** on the Wild Odds website (it opens Stripe's billing
portal). Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Verified Creator access until the end of
the period you've already paid for. No partial-month refunds are given for change of mind, but
nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law — see the Refunds
Policy.

## When a payment fails or you cancel

If a renewal payment fails, Stripe retries it; while the subscription is past due or after it
ends, the badge is removed and your marketplace listings are unpublished. Resubscribing restores
both.

## Price changes

If the price changes, we'll give you at least **30 days' notice** by email before it affects
you, and the new price only applies from your next renewal after that notice. If you don't want
to continue, cancel before the renewal.
