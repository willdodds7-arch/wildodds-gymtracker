This agreement applies when you sell training programs through the Wild Odds Gym Tracker
marketplace as a Verified Creator. It sits alongside the Terms of Service, the Subscription &
Billing Terms and the Refunds Policy. You must accept it before you can publish a listing.

## What you get and what it costs

- Selling requires an active **Verified Creator** subscription and a completed Stripe payout
  account. If your subscription lapses, your listings are unpublished until you resubscribe.
- On every sale, the platform keeps a **10% platform fee** and **you receive 90%** of the sale
  price. Card-processing costs (Stripe's fees) are deducted from your 90% share.
- The fee split is shown transparently on every sale in your earnings view.

## Payouts

Payouts are handled entirely by **Stripe Connect**. Stripe pays your share directly to the bank
account you connect during onboarding, on Stripe's normal payout schedule. We never hold your
money and do not operate a wallet. Identity (KYC) checks are Stripe's, not ours — we can't see
your identity documents.

## Your content

- You keep ownership of the programs you create.
- You grant us a non-exclusive, worldwide licence to host, display, market and deliver your
  published programs to buyers, for as long as they're listed and, for past buyers, for as long
  as they retain access.
- You warrant that you own (or are licensed for) everything in your programs, that they don't
  infringe anyone's rights, and that they contain no medical claims presented as medical advice.

## Your responsibilities

- **Tax is yours.** You are responsible for your own tax affairs, including income tax and — if
  you are (or become) required to register — **GST** on your sales. We recommend getting advice
  from an accountant; nothing in the app is tax advice.
- Keep your programs accurate and safe: no dangerous loading protocols, no guaranteed-results
  claims, no content targeting minors.
- Handle buyer questions about your program content in good faith.

## Removal and termination

We may unpublish or remove a program (and, for serious or repeated breaches, revoke selling
rights) if it breaches this agreement, the Terms of Service, or applicable law; if it attracts
substantiated complaints; or if it appears fraudulent. Where reasonable we'll tell you why and
give you a chance to fix it. You can unpublish your own listings at any time; existing buyers
keep access to what they bought.

## Liability

The platform is an intermediary: the sale contract for a program is between **you** and the
buyer. To the extent permitted by law (and without excluding the consumer guarantees under the
Australian Consumer Law, which cannot be excluded), we're not liable for your content or for
outcomes buyers get from your programs, and our total liability to you under this agreement is
capped at the platform fees we retained from your sales in the previous 12 months.
