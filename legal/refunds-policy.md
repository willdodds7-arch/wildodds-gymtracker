## Your rights come first

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies the **consumer guarantees under the
Australian Consumer Law** (or equivalent protections where you live). Those guarantees cannot be
excluded. If a product we supply fails a consumer guarantee — for example, a purchased program
is corrupted, can't be delivered to your app, or is substantially not what its listing
described — you're entitled to a remedy: a replacement, a fix, or a refund, as the law provides.

## Verified Creator subscription

- Change of mind: cancel any time; the subscription simply doesn't renew again. We don't give
  partial-month refunds for change of mind.
- Our fault (billed after you cancelled, double-charged, the service was materially unavailable
  for an extended period): contact Support and we'll refund the affected charge.

## Program purchases

- Programs are digital content delivered immediately, so we don't refund **change of mind** or
  "I didn't end up using it".
- We **do** refund where a consumer guarantee fails: broken or undeliverable content, a listing
  that materially misdescribed the program, or a duplicate charge.
- Ask via the Support page with your Stripe receipt. Refunds go back to the original payment
  method.

## What happens behind a refund

When a program purchase is refunded, the refund reverses the whole flow: the buyer gets the full
sale price back, the platform returns its 10% fee, and the creator's 90% share is reversed from
their Stripe account. The purchase's marketplace access is revoked.
