# Accessibility Statement

**Last updated 5 October 2026**

We want Wild Odds Gym Tracker to be usable by everyone.

## What we do

- **Screen readers:** buttons and controls carry spoken labels for TalkBack.
- **Text size:** the app follows your phone's font-size setting.
- **Contrast:** text and controls are designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast in light and dark themes.
- **Keyboard and switch access:** fields move to the next field with the keyboard's Next key, and
  every control is a standard, focusable Android control.
- **Light and dark themes** in Settings.

## Known limitations

Charts are visual. Each one has a text caption, TalkBack reads a summary of it (range, first and
latest value), and tapping or dragging on a chart shows the exact value for any week.

## Tell us

If something doesn't work for you, email **wildoddssupport@gmail.com** and we'll fix it.
